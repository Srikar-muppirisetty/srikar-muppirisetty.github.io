About

My work has moved between research and development, from telecommunications and signal processing to machine learning for automotive applications.

Over more than 17 years, I have worked with classical machine learning, forecasting, natural language processing and computer vision. I have also led teams, mentored colleagues and supervised academic work.

Selected work

Service and claim verification

Worked on natural-language processing for warranty claims and on approaches using generative AI to interpret and categorize claim text.

Generative AI · LLMs · Multi-agent systems · MLOps

Production NLP and model engineering

Transitioned a notebook-based warranty-claims solution into a maintainable system on the in-house ML platform, reducing its memory footprint from 64 GB to 40 MB while maintaining accuracy and performance.

NLP · Production ML · Model optimization

Forecasting and predictive systems

Served as technical lead for customer-demand prediction, including large-scale and hierarchical time-series forecasting for commercial operations.

Forecasting · Machine Learning · Analytics

Automotive safety applications

Contributed to road-friction estimation, slippery-road alerts, pothole detection, rear emergency braking and statistical sensor models for virtual testing.

Active Safety · Vehicle data · Computer Vision

Research & academic work

Research remains an important part of my professional work.

My PhD research at Chalmers University of Technology applied machine learning to wireless communications. The work covered wireless-channel prediction with uncertain location information, resource allocation using optimization and control, and pilot assignment for Massive MIMO. Research visits included Bell Laboratories in Murray Hill and The Ohio State University.
